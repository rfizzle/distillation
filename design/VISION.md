# Distillation — Vision

> *"Every drop counts."* — the brewing overhaul: a stand that teaches its own
> recipes, and potions worth the bottle they come in.

## The promise

Distillation takes over the brewing stand and everything it pours. The stand
teaches its own recipes, so you learn brewing by brewing instead of by
alt-tabbing to a wiki. Every effect vanilla ships becomes brewable, utility
potions last long enough to plan an evening around, and a cure can strip one
affliction instead of everything you drank. If you built one stand, brewed
one round of potions, and never lit it again, this mod is for you.

## The arc of a world

Your first stand is a conversation: hold an ingredient over it, watch the
vapor cloud with a color hint, gamble, and bottle a Murky Draught whose label
teaches you one ingredient that would have worked. Recipe by recipe your
recipes page fills in, and it never forgets. By mid game you brew effects
vanilla never wrote down and keep antidotes stocked for cave dives; late game
you kit a raid party in one sitting and throw lingering clouds from the back
line.

## The features, as you'll play them

### Brew by discovery
- **What it is** — The stand teaches its own recipes; no wiki required.
- **How you use it** — Hold an ingredient over the stand and the brew clouds
  with a color hint. A failed combination bottles a Murky Draught whose
  tooltip names one ingredient that would have worked — and drinking it,
  through 15 seconds of nausea, gives you a 20-second taste of the potion it
  wanted to be. Every success is written permanently into the recipes page
  of your brewing screen; land the last one and the count gilds — every
  drop, accounted for.
- **What changes for you** — Brewing becomes a system you learn by playing.
  Your first Night Vision costs a few murky bottles, not a browser tab. And a
  recipe you have learned can be copied onto paper — a note to sell, gift, or
  leave in a chest, so a server's master brewer teaches instead of dictating
  from the stand. The note points; the reader still brews it once to learn it.

### The missing brews
- **What it is** — Every effect vanilla shipped without a recipe becomes
  brewable from ingredients you already gather, and vanilla's two dead-end
  bottles finally get jobs.
- **How you use it** — Brew them like any potion: Resistance from a shulker
  shell, Haste from honey and sugar, Absorption from a golden apple. Luck
  from a nautilus shell is the angler's brew — junk leaves your line,
  treasure climbs onto it, and it stacks with Luck of the Sea. Glowing from
  a glow ink sac is a marker made for throwing: splash it on what you can't
  afford to lose track of in the dark. A Mundane Potion ferments into
  Weakness, and the Thick Potion is where every antidote begins.
- **What changes for you** — The effect roster vanilla already ships stops
  being creative-mode-only, and nothing the stand teaches leads nowhere.

### Batch brewing
- **What it is** — Recipes you have discovered scale past three bottles.
- **How you use it** — Set the stand on a cauldron heated by fire or a
  campfire and brew a recipe from your recipes page: one pass fills 6
  bottles at 1.5× the ingredient cost.
- **What changes for you** — Kitting a group for a raid takes one session,
  not six. Undiscovered recipes still brew three — the shortcut is earned.

### The stand talks to redstone
- **What it is** — A comparator against a brewing stand reads brew *state*,
  not just how full it is.
- **How you use it** — Set a comparator against the stand: it reads low while
  a cycle runs and high once the bottles are done, with the bottle count
  carried in the number — up to six on a rigged batch. Wire a lamp or a bell
  to the done reading and it fires the moment a pass finishes.
- **What changes for you** — "The batch is done" bell no longer means watching
  the bubbles yourself; the stand is finally legible to the automation you
  already build in vanilla.

### Draughts and honest durations
- **What it is** — Durations sized to how people play, and potions drinkable
  in halves.
- **How you use it** — Utility potions (Fire Resistance, Water Breathing,
  Night Vision) base at 8:00 instead of 3:00; combat potions keep their short
  timers. Sneak-drink to sip half: 4:00 now, a stoppered 4:00 for later.
- **What changes for you** — A potion becomes a commitment you plan around,
  not a timer that expires in transit.

### Both, not either/or
- **What it is** — Redstone and glowstone stop being mutually exclusive.
- **How you use it** — Add both, at double the base ingredient — two blaze
  powder for Strength instead of one — for an extended *and* amplified brew:
  Strength II for 4:00 instead of II at 1:30 or I at 8:00.
- **What changes for you** — The top end of brewing gets a real cost curve
  instead of a forced tradeoff.

### Antidotes
- **What it is** — Surgical cures, brewed from the affliction's own source.
- **How you use it** — Brew a Thick Potion — glowstone's body carries the
  cure — then add the affliction's own source: a fermented spider eye for
  poison, a wither rose for wither; each strips exactly that effect and
  nothing else. Milk still clears everything — your buffs included.
- **What changes for you** — Keeping your 8-minute Strength while shaking
  off a poison is a brewing decision, not a sacrifice.

### Lingering worth throwing
- **What it is** — Splash and lingering potions rebalanced as support tools.
- **How you use it** — Lingering clouds last 60 seconds instead of 30 and
  open at a 4½-block radius instead of 3; a splash potion loses an eighth of
  the drinkable duration instead of a quarter.
- **What changes for you** — A dedicated brewer is a viable support role:
  the cloud is still working when the second wave arrives.

### Tipped arrows worth fletching
- **What it is** — Tipped arrows craftable at survival prices, not gated
  behind a dragon's-breath lingering potion.
- **How you use it** — Right-click a water cauldron with a potion you have
  discovered to charge it, then dip a stack of arrows to tip eight at a time;
  each dip spends one water level. Vanilla's lingering-potion recipe still
  works alongside it.
- **What changes for you** — Arrows of Slowness or Poison become part of the
  brewing you already do, not a dragon's-breath luxury.

## Staying in control

Every piece switches off on its own — discovery hints, Murky Draughts, recipe
notes, batch brewing, the comparator read, the new recipes, the longer
durations, cauldron-tipped arrows, the splash and lingering rebalance — so a
server can run any slice of
the mod, or start everyone with the full recipes page unlocked. Change nothing and the
defaults are exactly this document; whatever you change, every vanilla recipe
still brews, milk still cures all, and golden apples are untouched. Discoveries
are per player
— your recipes page fills only with what you have brewed.

## Better together

- **Tribulation** — the afflictions its hardened mobs inflict gain brewable
  antidotes: a harder world, stocked up against. Never required.
- **Mercantile** — the cleric's stock deepens as your reputation rises: rare
  brewing ingredients for emeralds. Never required.
- **Prosperity** — far chests turn up reagents you have never held over a
  stand; a Potion of Luck is worth drinking first. Never required.
- **Meridian** — the two magics share manners — the table gives clues, the
  stand gives hints — and neither borrows the other's power. Never required.

With no siblings installed, nothing is missing.

## What Distillation will never do

- **Never touch enchanting.** Tables, books, and enchanted gear are
  Meridian's; potion and enchantment stay separate arts.
- **Never edit a villager's trades.** What the cleric sells is Mercantile's
  business; Distillation only makes items worth selling.
- **Never redefine another mod's afflictions.** Tribulation's debuffs stay
  its own; at most Distillation brews the cure.
- **Never change what anything drops.** Witch drops and chest loot belong to
  the world and to Prosperity.
- **Never add what you can't already find.** No new ingredients, crops, mobs,
  or dimensions — and never a recipe removed: every vanilla brew keeps working.
- **Never require a companion or claim your screen.** Alone it is complete;
  effects keep their vanilla icons and your HUD stays untouched.
